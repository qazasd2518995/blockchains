# BONUS
Credits times the total bet.

# PAY TABLE
All wins shown with bet multiplier at 1.

# MULTIPLIER FEATURE
These are multiplier symbols.
- Whenever a multiplier symbol hits, it takes a random multiplier value from 2x to 1000x.
- When cascading ends, the values of all multiplier symbols on the screen are added together and the total win is multiplied by the final value.

# SUPER BONUS
Trigger the Free Spin Feature and land Super Bonus symbols at the same time to earn multiplier wins based on how many appear.
- Landing 1 Super Bonus symbol awards 100x bet win.
- Landing 2 Super Bonus symbol awards 500x bet win.
- Landing 3 Super Bonus symbol awards 5000x bet win.
- Landing 4 Super Bonus symbol awards 25000x bet win.

# FREE SPIN FEATURE
Any 4, 5 or 6 Bonus Symbol on the reels initiate 15 Free Spins respectively.
- During Free Spin Feature, whenever a multiplier symbol hit and the spin results in a win, the multiplier value gets added to the total multiplier.
- For the whole duration of the round, whenever any new multiplier symbol hits and results in a win the total multiplier value is also used to multiply the win.
- Additional 5 free spins may be awarded with 3, 4, 5 or 6 Bonus Symbols during Free Spin Feature to a maximum of 100 free spins.

# MULTIPLIER UPGRADE FEATURE
- In the Free Spin Feature, when a multiplier symbol appears and the player scores, there is a chance to trigger an upgrade of the multiplier symbol before it is applied and added to the score.
- There is a chance to trigger multiple upgrades when a multiplier symbol is upgraded. Each multiplier symbol can reach up to 1000×.
- In the Super Free Spin Feature, the chance to trigger multiplier symbol upgrades will be increased.

# MAX WIN
The maximum winning points are 25000x the current bet.
- In the base game, when the winning points on screen has reached the upper limit, the current spin will automatically be ended with rewards distributed.
- In the  Free Spin Feature, when the winning points on screen has reached the upper limit, Free Spin will be automatically ended with rewards distributed.

# BUY FEATURE
The Free Spin Feature can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
During Free Spin Feature, the total bet credits remain the same as the spin that triggered Free Spin Feature.
When Free Spin Feature is triggered, press spin to play automatically until all free spins are used and wins are paid.

# SUPER BUY FEATURE
The Super Free Spin Feature can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 500x current total bet.
During Super Free Spin Feature, the total bet credits remain the same as the spin that triggered Super Free Spin Feature.
When Super Free Spin Feature is triggered, press spin to play automatically until all free spins are used and wins are paid.

# EXTRA BET
The extra bet is 25% of the original.When activated, the chance to enter free spin doubles.
The additional bet is not included in prize calculations. All prizes are calculated based on the original base bet.

# LUCKYSTRIKE
In the base game, when spinning with 4000× the bet amount, all appearing multiplier symbols will be 1000×.
During Lucky Strike Feature, the total bet credits remain the same as the spin that triggered Lucky Strike Feature.
When Lucky Strike Feature is triggered, press spin to play automatically until the round is completed and wins are paid.
The observed purchase confirmation states that this feature plays one spin, returns either no win or max win, and has an approximately 1-in-6.44 max-win chance per spin.

# PAY ANYWHERE GENERAL RULES
Minimum bet 20 credits.
- The total number of same symbol on the screen at the end of a spin determines the value of the win.
Malfunction voids all plays and pays.
